YOUR RESULTS APPEAR IN THIS FOLDER
======================================================================

Each video gets its own subfolder, named after the video file. For a
recording called VID_20250723_130058.mp4 you get:

    output/
      VID_20250723_130058/
        00_READ_ME_FIRST.txt          a guide to these files
        01_audio.mp3                  the sound on its own
        02_transcript.txt             who said what, with times
        03_subtitles.srt              subtitles for a video player
        04_narrative.txt              account of the video      (optional)
        05_narrative_by_section.md    the same, in short sections
        data/                         keep this to re-run a step later
        work/                         temporary files, safe to delete


WHERE TO START
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Open 02_transcript.txt. It looks like this:

    [00:12:00] Person1:
        ...no tienen por que cobrarle, le tienen que cobrar a partir de
        que muestre su recibo.

    [00:12:16] Person2:
        Nada mas deben el 25, paguen el 25.

The number in square brackets is the position in the original video.
Type it into your video player and you land on that exact moment.


HOW TO USE THE SUBTITLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Put 03_subtitles.srt in the same folder as your video, give it the same
name as the video file, and most players will load it automatically. Or
open it directly: VLC, "Subtitle" menu, "Add Subtitle File".

This is the fastest way to check a transcript: you read and listen at the
same time.


BEFORE YOU RELY ON ANY OF THIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

These files were produced automatically. They contain mistakes.

  - Names are almost always wrong the first time they are heard.
  - Figures get confused: 4900 and 49, "el 24" and "el 25".
  - Speaker labels can split one person into two. When the program is
    unsure it says so, in the file header and on screen while it runs.
  - The visual description can misread small print on a badge or a
    document.

Every line has a timecode precisely so that checking is quick. Open the
video at that second and confirm anything you intend to quote.

This is a drafting aid. It is not a certified transcript and it is not a
substitute for a court reporter.


DISK SPACE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The "work" folder inside each result can be deleted at any time. The
"data" folder is small and worth keeping: it lets you redo the speaker
labelling, or resume an interrupted description, without transcribing the
video again.
